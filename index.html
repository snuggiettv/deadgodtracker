Settings

Sync between devices off

Tick on your phone, in Meld or in any browser, and the overlay follows within a second or two. No account needed: progress goes through ntfy.sh, a free public relay, under a random room name. Anyone who has your room link can see and change your progress, so keep it to yourself.

Already have a room code (for example from the streamer you help)?

Room code

Editor link: open it on your phone or other browsers, or send it to a mod so they can tick progress for you during runs. Anyone with it can change your progress, so share it only with people you trust. Never put it on stream.

The overlay link on the Overlay setup tab uses the same room. It's view-only.

Backup & move between devices

Reset

Clears all marks, achievements and items. Your settings stay. If sync is on, the reset also reaches your overlay and other devices. You get 15 seconds to undo.

Build your overlay link

Views (tick more than one to rotate between them)

OBS (ticks show up live)

  1. Add a Browser source (width 400, height 300 for the summary) and paste the overlay link. Tick Shutdown source when not visible off.
  2. Go to Docks → Custom Browser Docks, name it "Dead God", and paste this page's link.
  3. Tick things in the dock. OBS docks and browser sources share storage, so the overlay updates within a second or two.

Meld Studio / other apps

Turn on Sync between devices (Items & settings tab) first, then add the overlay link as a browser/web layer. Ticks from your phone or any browser then show up live.
